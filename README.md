# 🧛 Twilight Saga

**Twilight** is a romantic fantasy series written by *Stephenie Meyer*, centered around the love story between a human girl and a vampire.

## 🌟 Why I Love Twilight

- **Unique supernatural romance** between Bella and Edward
- **Mysterious setting** in Forks, Washington
- **Werewolves vs. Vampires** conflict adds thrilling tension
- **Themes of love, choice, and identity**

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧛 Main Characters

| Character       | Actor/Actress       | Description                          |
|-----------------|---------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Bella Swan      | Kristen Stewart     | A human girl drawn into a vampire world |
| Edward Cullen   | Robert Pattinson    | A vampire who falls for Bella        |
| Jacob Black     | Taylor Lautner      | A werewolf and Bella’s close friend  |
| Alice Cullen    | Ashley Greene       | Edward’s psychic vampire sister      |

## 📚 Book Series

1. **Twilight** – Where it all begins  
2. **New Moon** – Heartbreak and werewolves  
3. **Eclipse** – Love triangle intensifies  
4. **Breaking Dawn** – Marriage, transformation, and final battle
